About Me.

Behind Fanuzi Studio Hi, I’m Regynox. At my core, I am a builder, a logical thinker, and a creative mind fascinated by how technology can simplify lives and bring ideas into the physical and digital world. My path into web development and technology has been driven by continuous learning and adaptability. From diving deep into computer science fundamentals and complex technical problem-solving to working hands-on with data annotation, quality assurance, and technical engineering, I’ve developed a keen eye for structure, precision, and efficiency. Fanuzi Studio represents my passion for merging technical logic with creative design. I don't just write code to make things function; I craft digital spaces that feel alive, engaging, and purposeful. When I’m not in my editor tweaking CSS or structuring web layouts, you’ll find me exploring emerging tech trends, sharing ideas through creative projects, and constantly challenging myself with new skills. I’m always open to new opportunities, technical collaborations, and ambitious projects—let’s build something amazing together!
