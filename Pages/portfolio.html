About Me.

Behind Fanuzi Studio Hi, I’m Regynox. At my core, I am a builder, a logical thinker, and a creative mind fascinated by how technology can simplify lives and bring ideas into the physical and digital world. When I’m not in my editor tweaking CSS or structuring web layouts, you’ll find me exploring emerging tech trends, sharing ideas through creative projects, and constantly challenging myself with new skills. I’m always open to new opportunities, technical collaborations, and ambitious projects—let’s build something amazing together!
